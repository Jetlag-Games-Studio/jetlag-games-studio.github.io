About us

Jetlag Games is a small independent game studio based in France. We make colourful, easy-to-pick-up mobile games built for quick sessions — and for that “just one more run” feeling.

Our first title, Keep Drifting, is an arcade drifting game: slide through endless roads, chain your drifts, dodge the traffic and climb the leaderboards against players around the world.
