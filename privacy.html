This policy explains what personal data the mobile game Keep Drifting (the “Game”) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Game as distributed on Google Play and the App Store.

By playing the Game you accept the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not play.

Jetlag Games, a French société par actions simplifiée (SAS) registered under SIREN 130 389 638 (“we”, “us”, “our”), is the developer and publisher of the Game and the party responsible for the data described here (the “data controller” under the GDPR).

For any privacy question or request, email jetlaggames.studio@gmail.com.

We never access your photos, camera or contacts.

4. How we use it

We use the data above only for these purposes. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is shown in brackets.

  • To run the Game’s features — cloud save, leaderboards, duels and weekly events. (Performance of our agreement with you.)
  • To keep the Game working and secure — compatibility, crash fixing, and preventing cheating, fraud and abuse. (Our legitimate interests.)
  • To understand usage and improve the Game — analytics on how modes, difficulty and progression perform. (Our legitimate interests; your consent where the law requires it.)
  • To show rewarded advertising — optional video ads you can choose to watch. (Your consent where the law requires it — see section 5.)
  • To show your public player profile — so other players can see your nickname, avatar and game stats on leaderboards. (Performance of our agreement with you.)
  • To send you gameplay reminders — local notifications on your device, only if you allowed notifications. (Your consent, given through your device’s notification permission.)

We do not use your data for automated decisions that produce legal effects, and we do not build advertising profiles ourselves.

5. Advertising

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads only: an ad plays only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a revive or doubled gems). The Game does not show ads you have not chosen to watch.

Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, which may use your Advertising ID and ad-interaction data to select and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted.

Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before any advertising or analytics identifier is used, through a consent prompt shown on first launch. The prompt is provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform, and operates within the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). Until you have answered, no optional analytics are collected and ads are not personalised. Your choice is shared, in a standardised form, with the advertising partners in the mediation so they process your data accordingly.

You can accept, or refuse or limit the use of your data:

  • If you accept, ads may be personalised and their performance measured, and gameplay analytics is enabled.
  • If you refuse, you will still be able to play and to watch rewarded ads, but those ads will be non-personalised (selected from context only), and we do not collect optional gameplay analytics.

Your choice is stored on your device and applied to every future session. You can change it at any time from the Settings screen in the Game (ad privacy options), which re-opens the same prompt. You can also reset your Advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the ad mediation described above. We share data only with:

  • Unity Technologies — Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Remote Config, Analytics) and Unity LevelPlay, acting as our service provider. → unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • ironSource / Unity LevelPlay and the advertising networks in its mediation — for ad serving and measurement. → is.com/privacy-policy
  • Google — Google Play Games Services (sign-in), Google Play distribution, and the User Messaging Platform (consent prompt, configured through Google AdMob). → policies.google.com/privacy
  • Other players — your nickname, avatar, scores and public profile stats are visible in the Game (leaderboards and profile cards).
  • Authorities — only where we are legally required to disclose data.

7. International transfers

Unity and Google process data on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards set out in those providers’ own privacy policies, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how it is used, to receive a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of the EEA/UK have these rights under the GDPR; residents of California have comparable rights under the CCPA/CPRA.

  • Advertising and analytics consent — manage or withdraw it through the consent prompt, in game settings, or through your device’s ad settings.
  • Your nickname and avatar — you can change them inside the Game at any time; your public profile is updated automatically.
  • Notifications — you can refuse the notification permission, or turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Access to your data — to receive a copy of the data we hold about you, contact us as described in section 13.
  • Everything else, including deletion — contact us as described in the next section.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your player account and all data associated with it at any time, for any reason.

Email us from any address, with the subject line “Delete my account”: jetlaggames.studio@gmail.com

or use Settings → Delete my account inside the Game for immediate deletion.

We will delete your Unity player account, your cloud save (including your public player profile), your leaderboard entries and your analytics data, and confirm by reply — normally within 30 days. Deleting the Game from your device on its own does not delete this data.

If your account is anonymous (you never signed in with Google Play Games), tell us your in-Game nickname and, if you can, your approximate last play date so we can identify the right record.

10. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you stop playing without asking for deletion, data tied to an anonymous account may remain in Unity’s systems until you request its removal. Analytics data is retained according to Unity’s standard retention schedule and is aggregated over time.

11. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age of digital consent in their country, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be signaled in the Game or on its store listing. The Effective date at the top always reflects the current version.
